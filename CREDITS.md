- J-Type sprites, backgrounds, effects and logos: original 16-bit set made for the project.
- J-Type sounds and music: CC0 files from OpenGameArt, by Juhani Junkala and MintoDog. The files and their sources are listed in [the J-Type architecture page](j-type/docs/architecture/index.md#where-the-sounds-come-from).

- Younity Engine logos: original.
