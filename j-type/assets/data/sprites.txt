# The sprites of the game, one animation per line, read by the client.
# <name> <path> <width> <height> <columns> <first> <count> <duration> <loop|once> <origin x> <origin y> <layer>
# The frames of a sheet are <width> x <height> pixels, numbered from 0, left to right then top to bottom,
# <columns> on a row. The animation shows <count> frames from <first>, each one <duration> seconds,
# then starts again (loop) or stays on the last one (once). The origin is the point of the frame put on the
# position of the entity. The higher layers are drawn over the lower ones: 0 projectiles, 1 Bydos and
# scenery, 2 ships, 3 effects, 4 interface.
# The background layers are not here: the starfield scrolls them whole.
