# The waves of Bydos of a game, played in order, then again faster; beating the boss of a wave wins the game.
# "wave <pause>" starts a wave <pause> seconds after the last Bydo of the wave before is destroyed or gone.
# Each line after it brings a group: <type> <pattern> <count> <delay between two, in seconds> [<formation> <height>]
# [drop], an obstacle: "wall <top> <bottom>" or "zapper <top> <bottom>", or the boss: "boss incubator".
# With drop, the last Bydo of the group leaves a power-up when it is shot. A wall of blocks breaks under the missiles,
# a zapper does not; both scroll with the stage, and their heights go from 0, the ceiling, to 1, the floor.
# Types: blobling, jelly, sentry_drone. Patterns: straight, sine_wave, chase.
# Formations: random (the default, without a height), line, column, v. The height goes from 0 (top) to 1 (bottom).
# A Blobling only flies, a Jelly shoots straight and a Sentry drone aims: the first waves bring one type at a time.
